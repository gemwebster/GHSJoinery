About GHS Joinery

GHS Joinery, established in 2026, is a Forfar based joinery business. We cover kitchen fitting, bathrooms, windows, doors and a wide range of general joinery including small repairs.

With over 20 years experience, we pride ourselves on quality workmanship and reliable service.

We cover Forfar, Arbroath, Kirriemuir, Dundee and throughout Angus, Tayside and the North East of Scotland.
