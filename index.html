About GHS Joinery

GHS Joinery, established in 2026, is a Forfar based joinery business. We cover kitchen fitting, bathrooms, windows, doors and a wide range of general joinery including small repairs.

With over 20 years experience, we focus on quality workmanship, reliability and customer satisfaction.

We serve Forfar, Arbroath, Kirriemuir, Dundee and the wider Angus, Tayside and North East Scotland region.
